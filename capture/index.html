    The five rules of a dataset that reconstructs
    1. Move, do not spin. Turning on the spot gives a panorama, and a panorama has no depth to triangulate. Every shot must come from a different place.
    2. Overlap. Two neighbouring shots should share most of what they see. Big jumps leave holes nothing can bridge.
    3. Go around. One side of a thing reconstructs one side of a thing. Rings at different heights are what close a model.
    4. Hold still enough. Motion blur destroys the corners the whole method is built on, so blurred frames are dropped — unless dropping one would leave a hole, because a hole is worse.
    5. Give it something to look at. A blank wall is in perfect focus and still impossible: there is nothing on it to match. Keep textured things in frame.
